## 1. Introduction

Secure Gate Access Control System ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our access control system.

This policy complies with the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019** and other applicable data protection laws.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information
We collect the following categories of personal information:

#### For Residents:
- **Identity Information**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Residential Information**: Unit/apartment number, building address
- **Authentication Data**: Username, encrypted password
- **Contact Information**: Emergency contact details

#### For Visitors:
- **Identity Information**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Visit Information**: Purpose of visit, expected arrival time, actual check-in/out times
- **Identification**: Government-issued ID details (when required)
- **Contact Information**: Host resident information

#### For Security Guards:
- **Identity Information**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Employment Information**: Badge number, shift schedule, employment status
- **Contact Information**: Emergency contact details

#### For Administrators:
- **Identity Information**: Full name, email address, phone number
- **Administrative Information**: Role, permissions, access levels

### 2.2 Technical Information
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, device identifiers
- **Usage Data**: System access logs, feature usage patterns
- **Security Data**: Login attempts, security events, audit trails
- **Location Data**: Building access points, check-in/out locations

### 2.3 Automatically Collected Information
- **System Logs**: Server logs, error logs, performance metrics
- **Security Events**: Failed login attempts, suspicious activities
- **Access Patterns**: Time and frequency of system access

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
We retain your personal data for the following periods:

#### Active Users:
- **Resident Data**: Retained while account is active + 1 year
- **Visitor Data**: Retained for 90 days after last visit
- **Security Logs**: Retained for 1 year
- **Audit Logs**: Retained for 7 years (legal requirement)

#### Inactive Users:
- **Account Data**: Deleted 1 year after account closure
- **Visitor Records**: Deleted 90 days after last visit
- **System Logs**: Deleted according to retention schedule

### 6.2 Automated Deletion
- **Daily Cleanup**: Automated deletion of expired visitor records
- **Monthly Review**: Regular review of inactive accounts
- **Annual Audit**: Comprehensive data retention audit

### 6.3 Data Anonymization
Before deletion, we may anonymize data for:
- **Statistical Analysis**: System usage and performance metrics
- **Research Purposes**: Improving access control systems
- **Compliance Reporting**: Meeting regulatory requirements

## 10. Data Breach Notification

### 10.1 Our Response
In the event of a data breach, we will:
- **Immediate Assessment**: Evaluate the scope and impact within 24 hours
- **Containment**: Take immediate steps to contain the breach

- **Regulatory Reporting**: Report to the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours
- **Remediation**: Implement measures to prevent future breaches

### 10.2 Your Response
If you suspect a data breach or unauthorized access:
- **Immediate Contact**: Contact us immediately at security@securegate.com
- **Password Change**: Change your password immediately
- **Monitor Accounts**: Monitor your accounts for suspicious activity
- **Report Suspicious Activity**: Report any unusual system behavior

## 15. Glossary

**Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual  
**Processing**: Any operation performed on personal data  
**Controller**: The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing  
**Processor**: The entity that processes data on behalf of the controller  
**Consent**: Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous agreement  
**Data Subject**: The individual whose personal data is being processed  
**Data Breach**: A security incident leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of personal data
